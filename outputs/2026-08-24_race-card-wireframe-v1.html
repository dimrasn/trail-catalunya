What we accent, and what we demote

1Name + verdict + difficultyACCENTThe “is this my race?” answer. One sentence anyone can grasp, plus the difficulty scale. This is the page's job — today it's a 3-line footnote.
2The gateGROUPDrive · distances · date + status chips (sold-out, night, entry-open). The cost side of your “worth-the-drive” call — grouped, color-cued, not a billboard.
3Detail + characterPRESENTPer-distance table + character. Earns its place with detail the strip can't hold; visually calmer so it never competes with tier 1.
3RegisterANCHOROne clear action, one disclaimer. On mobile it can become a sticky bottom bar.

One idea worth deciding early (feeds the design system): let color carry meaning consistently, so the card is readable at arm's length. Difficulty runs a fixed spectrum Easy→Brutal, and the drive number reuses the same warm ramp against your 1-hour instinct — ≤1h green, ~1–1.5h amber, >1.5h red “needs to be worth it”. Same colors = one visual language for “how demanding” and “how far”, and anyone learns to read a card in a glance.
